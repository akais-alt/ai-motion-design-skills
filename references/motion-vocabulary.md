# Motion Vocabulary

## Timing
anticipation, impact, hold, release, settle, follow-through, stagger, rhythm, pause.

## Easing
linear, ease-in, ease-out, ease-in-out, cubic-bezier, spring, overshoot, damped spring.

## Spatial
translate, scale, rotate, skew, parallax, orbit, path-follow, mask, crop, reveal.

## Visual
blur, opacity, tracking, glow, grain, gradient, shadow, displacement, distortion.

## Camera
push-in, pull-out, pan, tilt, orbit, rack-focus simulation, handheld simulation, locked-off.

## Composition
rule of thirds, center lockup, diagonal axis, symmetry, asymmetry, negative space, depth layers.

## Principle
Choose the smallest vocabulary that communicates the idea clearly.
