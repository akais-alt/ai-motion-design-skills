# Social Video

## Default format

- 9:16
- 1080×1920 target
- mobile-first typography
- keep critical content inside safe areas

## Hook

The first seconds must answer:
- Why should I keep watching?
- What is surprising/useful/emotional?
- What question is being created?

Avoid long logo intros.

## Editing rhythm

Use visual changes when the information changes, not simply because time passed.

Combine:
- text emphasis
- punch-ins
- B-roll
- graphic overlays
- pattern interrupts
- progress indicators

## Captions

Captions must be:
- large enough for mobile
- high contrast
- synchronized
- limited to readable chunks
- visually consistent

## Output variants

When useful create:
- 9:16 master
- 1:1 adaptation
- 16:9 YouTube adaptation

Recompose rather than merely crop.
