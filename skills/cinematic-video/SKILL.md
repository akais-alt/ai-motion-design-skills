# Cinematic Video

## Workflow

### 1. Story
Define one sentence for the film, audience, emotional arc and call to action.

### 2. Structure
Use a clear sequence:
- Hook
- Context
- Escalation
- Payoff
- Brand/message close

Each scene must have a purpose.

### 3. Shot design
For every shot specify:
- subject
- framing
- camera movement
- focal depth
- lighting
- environment
- action
- duration
- transition

### 4. Timing
Prefer intentional rhythm:
- establish
- accelerate
- breathe
- peak
- resolve

Do not make every shot the same duration.

### 5. Cinematic language
Use motivated camera moves, depth, parallax, scale changes, foreground/background separation and controlled contrast.

Avoid fake cinematic effects that do not serve the story.

### 6. AI-generated media
Prompts should specify subject + environment + lens/camera + lighting + composition + motion + continuity constraints.

Maintain character, object, wardrobe and environment continuity across scenes.

### 7. Final validation
Check narrative clarity without audio, readability on a phone, continuity between shots, audio peaks, subtitle safe areas and final export specifications.
